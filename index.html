Run this from your phone, cast to a monitor

  1. Open this page on your phone. It is live at aa8corp.github.io/free-tv-demo/
  2. Full screen: tap Full screen at the top. On iPhone, Safari does not do full screen for a web page, so instead tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, open it from the home screen, and rotate to landscape. On Android Chrome, tap Full screen and it fills the monitor.
  3. Cast, iPhone: Control Center, Screen Mirroring, pick the Apple TV or AirPlay monitor. Or use a USB-C to HDMI adapter.
  4. Cast, Android: Quick Settings, Cast or Screen Cast, pick the Chromecast or TV. Or use a USB-C to HDMI adapter.
  5. In class: tap Games, tap a game card to expand it, tap Present mode for huge text on the monitor. Tap a channel category chip to filter channels, no typing.
  6. Refresh games: tap Refresh today's games from ESPN. That fetches live from ESPN in the phone browser, no server, no API key. If the venue Wi-Fi blocks it, the pre-run Oct 26 games stay on screen.

If the monitor shows the browser address bar, rotate the phone to landscape and scroll down once, or use Present mode, which hides the tabs.
